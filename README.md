# Qavex Canfield Cards Gold

Qavex Canfield Cards Gold takes a seat in the Monte Carlo Salon, a 1920s casino where the croupier Renard deals a random base card to open the foundations. Four columns, a 13-card reserve and draw-3 stock make this the gambler’s solitaire, across 7 modes, 24 puzzle salons and daily seeded tables.

- Package: `com.qavex.qavexcanfieldcardsgold`
- Paid, fully offline casual game by QAVEX (muhammad ehtasham).
- Zero ads, zero trackers. One optional "Buy Me a Coffee" IAP.

## Modes
- **Classic**: The pure game, no timer.
- **Timed Run**: Beat the clock for bonus points.
- **Daily Challenge**: One seeded deal per day. Streaks pay bonus.
- **Zen Table**: No timer, no pressure. Free hints and undo.
- **Kids Mode**: Gentle guidance and extra help.
- **Practice**: Learn with auto-hints.
- **Puzzle Levels**: 24 curated deals with par scores.
